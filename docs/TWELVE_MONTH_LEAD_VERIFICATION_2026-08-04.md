## Editorial safeguards applied

- Existing events were matched by date, location, organiser and grievance, not by keyword alone.
- Planned actions were not converted into occurred events.
- Silence after an event was not treated as evidence that it ended or remained active.
- Government, police and political claims are attributed and are not presented as adjudicated fact.
- The four July NEET-related records remain separate location-specific actions; they are not merged
  merely because they share an issue.
- The nationwide 12 February strike is one national event cluster; state reports are supporting
  manifestations, not duplicate event records.
- Serious allegations and communal-risk material remain held for enhanced evidence and
  right-of-reply review.
- No media is approved and no public event or status is changed by this packet.
