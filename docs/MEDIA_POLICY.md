# Media policy

India Observed uses visuals to help readers navigate records without weakening the distinction
between evidence and illustration. A visual classification never changes an event's verification
status.

## Evidence classes

Every published visual has one controlled evidence class:

- **Verified event media** is authenticated media matched to the event and cleared through
  integrity, privacy, safety, rights and human-editorial review.
- **Context media** provides relevant background but does not depict the recorded event. The
  interface must say: “Context image — does not depict this event.”
- **Editorial illustration** is original India Observed artwork generated from controlled record
  metadata. It is navigation and editorial presentation, not event evidence.

Context media and illustrations are not evidence because neither reliably establishes what
happened at the event. Visual classifications do not affect event verification status.

## Acceptable rights bases

Media may be published only under explicit permission, an official publisher or platform embed, a
verified CC BY or CC BY-SA licence, CC0, public-domain status, or India Observed ownership.

A source article is not automatically a media licence. A search-engine image result is never
sufficient provenance. An `og:image` URL is not itself permission. No image is added solely because
a card needs visual variety.

An official embed remains hosted and controlled by its publisher or platform. India Observed does
not download, screenshot, proxy, cache, re-upload, crop or extract unofficial streams from embedded
media. Publisher media is click-to-load and does not autoplay.

## Attribution and licence verification

Editors must retain the creator or publisher, original source URL, rights basis, review date,
attribution text and, for open media, the licence name and deed URL. Before approving open-licensed
media, the reviewer verifies that the source identifies the creator, the licence applies to the
specific asset, attribution requirements can be met and the licence was valid at review time.

The event page displays the classification and credit beside the visual. Context media must disclose
that it does not depict the event. Original-source and licence links remain available to readers.

## Verification, privacy and safety

Event media requires authenticity, same-event and date verification, integrity review, privacy
review, safety review and human-editorial approval. Reviewers assess identifiable people, minors,
sensitive locations, faces, number plates, manipulation and cropping. Rights approval cannot
override a failed authenticity, event-match, privacy or safety gate. Auto-publication is disabled.

Editorial illustrations may use only controlled metadata. They must not simulate documentary
photography or depict people, violence, injuries, arrests, deaths, weapons, exact crowds, logos,
flags, specific incidents or unverified details.

## Broken media and takedowns

If an external thumbnail fails, the interface makes one load attempt and then shows the event's
India Observed illustration with “Publisher thumbnail unavailable,” a source link and an explicit
fallback disclosure. The original media classification and publisher credit remain visible; the
fallback is never relabelled as evidence.

Takedown requests are recorded and reviewed by an editor. Where justified, India Observed removes or
withholds the display without erasing the correction trail, retains the internal decision and
reason, updates the public disclosure where necessary and confirms that cached or copied assets do
not remain under India Observed's control.

## Replacement workflow

An illustration may later be replaced by verified event media only after the candidate completes
provenance, same-event, integrity, privacy, safety, rights and human-editorial review. The media
registry is updated with the classification, rights basis, credit, source and review date. The
factual event record is not edited merely to change its visual.
